Huge Improvements for 2025!

New advanced warning signs! Pedestrians, cyclists, and drivers now have several days' notice that Waterfront Drive will be closed.

New detour signs through the Goldeyes pedestrian plaza! You no longer have to wonder how to get through. People on bikes are no longer asked to add 20 minutes to their journey.

Later closures: starting at 6pm! Rather than closing off Waterfront Drive at the start of the evening commute! The Goldeyes and Archangel Fireworks have a new plan to delay the closure as long as is practicable.

Big thanks to the Winnipeg Goldeyes for listening and taking action.
